恋 の 詩 · A POEM OF LOVE
Hamsafar
One Year Complete 17 / 09 / 2025  –  17 / 09 / 2026
Waqt guzarta gaya, aur humare wasl (milne) ki ghadi kareeb aati gayi, Is tareek (andheri) duniya mein, umeed ka ek ujaala aa hi gaya.
Jab bikhra uski tabish (chamak) ka jalwa, toh chaand ki roshni bhi pheeki pad gayi, Uski aankhon se duniya kitni haseen dikh sakti hai, ye main dekh chuka hoon, Aur ek baar phir is dil-e-nadan ko us par qurban kar chuka hoon.
Waqt sarakta raha, aur tanhai ki raatein ab dhalne lagi hain, Kyunki zindagi ki raahon mein ab ek sacha sathi milne laga hai.
Waqt ka wo khoobsurat daur dekha hai maine, ki ab zamane ki parwah nahi, Din se raat kab dhali, ab iska koi hosh nahi.
Humare is haseen sath ko ab ek saal mukammal (poora) ho gaya hai, Aur ab unke ghar walon ko bhi humare is rishte ki khabar hai.
Ab yaqeen hai ki saare khwab tabeer (sach) honge, Ek sacha hamsafar bankar wo hamesha mere sath hoga.
Uski har ek guftagu (baat) mein mera hi zikr chhupa hoga, Aur meri baaton mein bas use muskurane ka ek pyara sa andaaz hoga.
Humein ab kisi aur ki tamanna nahi is zindagi mein, Humein chahiye toh bas wo waqt... jo hamesha humare sath ka hoga.
Zindagi mein ab kisi ki parwah nahi, kyunki wo ek ba-kamaal insaan hai, Jo ghar ko sanwarne ke sath, khud ke qadmon par khada hona bhi jaanti hai.
Usko zindagi mein bulandiyon ko chhoona hai, main use kabhi tootne na doonga, Aur waqt ke sath main bhi poori tarah sirf usi ka hokar reh jaoonga.
Safar-e-zindagi mein humari mulaqat bhale hi na ke barabar ho, Magar mohbbat hamesha dilon-jaan se rahegi, chahe wo sirf ek raabte (call) par hi kyun na ho.
Mera aaj bhi tum ho, mera kal bhi tum ho... Jaane is kayanat (duniya) ka tum kaun sa haseen sach ho, Jo mere sath hamsafar bankar chale, tum wahi meri duaaon ka sacha asar ho.
愛
Hamsafar · A Love Woven In Time
